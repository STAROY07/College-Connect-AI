Student Welfare Schemes & Scholarships

Financial grants, book bank schemes, Yuva Raksha insurance, and entrepreneurship startup funds

🎁 JMF Scholarship

Rs. 500 to Rs. 10,000/-

Given to all students who deserve irrespective of caste and religion. Available to underprivileged and economically backward students based on social status of students and guardians.

💡 Applied directly through the college office counter with parental income certificate.

🛡️ Yuva Raksha Group Insurance

Premium: Rs. 40/- / Year

Introduced by University of Mumbai for students in affiliated colleges covering three accident contingencies:

  • 100% financial coverage in case of sad demise of student.
  • 100% financial coverage for permanent disability.
  • Coverage for hospitalization expenses arising out of accidents.

📚 Students' Aid Fund & Book Bank

Yearly Fee: Rs. 5/-

Students' Aid Fund: Provides financial grants to needy students for tuition/exam fees, S.T. bus pass, and medical charges.

Book Bank: Textbooks issued for the entire academic year to needy students, along with competitive exam study resources.

🚀 Student Start-Up Scheme

Seed Capital: Up to Rs. 5000/-

Seed capital provided by the college for students interested in launching small business ventures upon submitting a formal business proposal and plan.

Allocation and approval is under the sole discretion of the Principal.

🧠 Counseling Cell

Daily: 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Confidential guidance for students coping with stress, emotional strain, or personal difficulties with professional counselors.

All matters are kept strictly confidential. Contact Principal in this regard.
