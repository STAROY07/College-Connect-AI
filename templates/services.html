Student Extension Services

Official administrative desk services with committed processing times directly from the handbook

🚆 Railway Concession

Turnaround: 05 mins

Bonafide students below 25 years of age are eligible for railway season ticket concessions between the station nearest to their home and Kopar station.

Documents Required:
  • Prescribed application form with Name, Address, Class, Roll No., DOB
  • Attested photocopy of Ration Card or valid residence proof
  • Submission between recess & after lectures on working days

📄 Bonafide Certificate

Turnaround: 05 mins

Official institutional certificate for educational and administrative verifications.

Requirements:
  • Regular Students: Application with Name, Address, Class, Roll No, DOB, Academic Year (e.g. 2024-25)
  • Repeater Students: Application + Attested photocopy of last year's mark sheet
